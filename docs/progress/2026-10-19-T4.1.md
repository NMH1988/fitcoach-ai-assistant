- **Why:** the profile is stored once in kg and cm and only converted at input and display. A save rounds to the precision the form shows (0.1 kg or lb, whole cm or inch), except for a weight or height whose shown value was not changed (e.g. after a units switch, or with only another field edited): that keeps its stored kg or cm. Validation sits in `src/profile.py`, not in the widgets: widget limits are enforced in the browser only, and the tools in T4.2 need the same checks.
- **Decisions & gotchas:**
  - Owner's choices (2026-10-19): the form opens in an `st.dialog` (the open question in `docs/UI_DESIGN.md`); one Metric / Imperial switch; ranges 30-300 kg, 120-230 cm, 18-100 years; five activity levels with working labels and no factors yet; sex is male or female because the planned energy equation (Mifflin-St Jeor) has only those two forms. On PR #27 the owner confirmed that the switch in the dialog satisfies the criterion "the sidebar has a profile form and a units setting": no separate sidebar switch, and the tiles change units on Save.
  - The dialog is opened by a `session_state` flag and closed by `on_dismiss`, not by calling it from a button's `if`: with the button pattern, AppTest loses the dialog on the next rerun (checked), so it could not be tested.
  - The dialog uses plain widgets, not `st.form`: form values are not in `session_state` until submit, so switching units would drop what was typed.
  - Imperial bounds are the metric ones rounded as the form shows them (66.1-661.4 lb, 3 ft 11 in to 7 ft 7 in), and a value accepted that way is moved onto the metric bound. Without this, a 30 kg or 230 cm profile shown in imperial and saved again would be rejected.
  - Switching the form's units converts the typed values, which can move them by up to the rounding (179 cm -> 5 ft 10 in -> 178 cm). `switch_form_units()` remembers the last switch, so switching back with nothing edited restores exactly what was typed. A newly opened form starts with a memory of the stored profile in both systems (`initial_form_memory()`), so the first unedited switch shows the stored values and an unchanged save keeps them. The weight and the height are compared and restored separately, so an edit to one of them, or to another field, in between does not stop the exact restore of the other.
  - With the default `metricValueFontSize` (2.25rem) the sidebar tiles cut off "176.4 lb" and "5 ft 11 in"; hence the one theme line.
