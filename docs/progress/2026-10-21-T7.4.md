## 2026-10-21 · T7.4 German input guard and guardrails · #65 (closes #63)
- **Brief:** Hard 4 (multi-language support) as an extra after the core, with core 3 (security measures relevant to the domain) and Medium 3 (prompt injection protection) for German input; AC-16. Beyond the PRD's original English-only scope (owner's request, 2026-10-20).
- **What:**
  - `src/german.py` (new): `german(pattern)` lets each umlaut in a pattern also match the plain vowel and the typed ae/oe/ue.
  - `src/guard.py`: `_DE_FOLDED_PATTERNS`, the German families of the injection patterns (overrides with the verb first and last, "außer Kraft", "alles, was dir gesagt wurde", requests for the prompt, role changes, modes, "tu so, als ...", "nicht mehr gebunden", safety filters), compiled with the English ones; "System-Prompt" with a hyphen; the role-line pattern also takes "Assistent:", "Entwickler:", "Neue Anweisungen:" and "Systemnachricht:".
  - `src/guardrails.py`: German drug terms and use words (same sentence, as in English), minors, pregnancy and breastfeeding, diseases, side effects with their frames, medication, disordered eating, and German daily intakes below the floor (`_DE_LOW_INTAKE_PATTERNS`: verb first or last, the day named before or after the amount, "1.100" read as 1100), joined to the English lists by `_compiled()`.
  - Tests: `tests/test_guard_de.py` (69 attacks, 49 benign questions, the umlaut helper, no "ß" in any German pattern, timing with zero-width padding) and `tests/test_guardrails_de.py` (135 flagged strings over the five concerns, 110 benign questions plus the guard's 49, 14 English questions that must keep passing, the floor from config, an amount without a day, timing).
  - Docs: plan (T7.4 added, T7.1 depends on it, two criteria moved, the cut order, the owner's T7.1 choices), `docs/SECURITY.md` (both pattern points, a German limits point, "system-prompt"), `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` step 2, `docs/UI_DESIGN.md` (Safety line and the ticket table).
- **Why:** a German UI invites German input, so the German checks ship before it (owner's decision to split T7.1, 2026-10-21, as its size was closer to L than M). The German patterns copy the English families and their trade-offs (narrow on purpose, someone else's advice named right after the noun passes, the system prompt as the second line), so both languages behave alike and the English tests stay unchanged.

  - German puts the infinitive last, so each override comes in two orders, with at most three words between noun and verb. The formal "Ihre" is also "her" or "their", so it counts only after a formal imperative.

Kept on the safe side and documented: "Krebs" (also crab), "bei" + a side effect, "die Pille", "für Kinder" in any sense.
