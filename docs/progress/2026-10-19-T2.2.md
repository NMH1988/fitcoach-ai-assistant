## 2026-10-19 · T2.2 Parse article XML and chunk with metadata · #29 (closes #5)
- **Brief:** core requirement 1 (chunking). The correction link (#18a <-> #18) serves the copyright policy's rule 7 (beyond the brief, owner's request).
- **What:**
  - `src/rag/ingest.py` (new): `parse_article()` (JATS XML -> `ParsedArticle` with the title and `Section`s in reading order), `chunk_article()` (sections -> LangChain `Document`s with metadata), `load_chunks()` (manifest -> `data/raw/<PMCID>.xml` -> `check_article()` -> parse -> chunks; `IngestError` lists every missing, refused or empty article).
  - `src/config.py`: `CHUNK_SIZE` 1000, `CHUNK_OVERLAP` 150, `CHUNK_MIN_BODY_CHARS`, `MAIN_TEXT_TITLE`, and the back-matter `sec-type`s and titles to drop.
  - `src/rag/sources.py` and `data/sources.json`: optional `corrects` field (#18a corrects #18); a source cannot correct itself or an unlisted source.
  - Tests: `tests/test_ingest.py` with the made-up fixture `tests/fixtures/jats/ingest_article.xml` (every kept and dropped element, citation clean-up, text brackets left alone, inline formulas, exponents, MathML scripts, fences and accents, XML line breaks, nested lists, chunk size, overlap, section boundaries, title prefix, metadata, `chunk_index`, the correction link, missing/refused/empty files); `tests/test_sources_manifest.py` (the `corrects` field against the "Correction to #N" rows of `docs/SOURCES.md`, bad links rejected).
  - Docs: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 4 (parsing and chunking rules), `docs/TESTING.md` (real run), plan (T2.2 in review; T1.2, T1.3 and T2.1 marked done, their PRs #24, #25 and #26 had merged).
- **Why:** the licence check runs again before parsing, so a file swapped in `data/raw/` after the download cannot slip into the index. A failing article stops the build instead of being skipped, so no source silently goes missing from the index. Chunks are split per section with the title path in front, so each chunk says what it is about even when retrieved alone; the title counts towards the 1,000 characters so chunks stay within the embedding budget planned in section 4.
- **Decisions & gotchas:**
  - Owner's choices (2026-10-19): drop all back matter, also when it sits inside `<body>` (Europe PMC puts acknowledgements, funding, disclosures, publisher's notes, supplementary, even the reference list there as ordinary sections); inline formulas keep their plain text (statistics symbols in #15, #16, #19 and #20; there is no display formula) and the criterion's wording was changed to match; #18a and #18 point to each other in the metadata.
  - Back matter is recognised by `sec-type` or by title (lower case, numbering and punctuation removed; "appendix..." too). A new article with an unusual title for back matter would slip through: check the kept titles when a source is added (the T2.2 check printed them).
  - Numeric citation markers are removed so the model never confuses "[12]" with its own `[S#]` labels. Other cross-references ("Table 1") keep their text, so the model may see a mention of a table it cannot read (a note for T3.1's prompt). One citation written as plain text without markup is left (#3, `[189–192,194]`).
  - Formatting that carries meaning is written out, so numbers are not distorted (copyright policy rule 7): superscripts as exponents (`10^-3`, `kg^-1`, `I^2`, `Ca^2+`), MathML `msup`/`msub`/`mfenced`/accents as `x^2`, `β_x`, `P(>0)`, `R̂`. Whitespace inside MathML is ignored, and the XML's own line breaks never split a sentence. Two publisher quirks are handled and tested: a line break before an exponent (#16, `τ ^2`) and a hat repeated inside its base (#15).
  - Citation markers are replaced by a placeholder first, so only brackets that held markers are removed (nested ones too); brackets in the text (`(-)-epicatechin`, `f()`) are never touched. Only numeric markers are removed; an author-year citation keeps its words. Footnote markers (`xref` to `fn`/`table-fn`) are dropped with their notes, and a quotation credited with `<attrib>` is dropped as third-party content (none in today's articles).
  - Overlap: the splitter carries up to 150 characters over when it cuts inside a paragraph; at a paragraph break it carries nothing (642 of 1,318 neighbouring pairs in the real run), so no sentence is cut either way. The owner confirmed on 2026-10-19 that this meets "150 overlap" in the criterion.
  - Extra metadata is merged first, so it can never overwrite one of the ten required keys.
  - `authors` and `topics` are lists in the metadata (chromadb 1.5.9 accepts list values and `$contains` filters). `corrects` / `corrected_by` are only present when they apply, never `None`.
