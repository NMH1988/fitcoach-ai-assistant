## 2026-10-19 · T4.2 Energy and macro tools with the calorie floor · #30 (closes #12)
- **Brief:** core requirement 2 (tools 1 and 2), core requirement 3 (the calorie floor is a domain guardrail); AC-7. O3 done.
- **What:**
  - `src/tools/energy.py` (new): `calculate_tdee(weight_kg, height_cm, age, sex, activity)` returns BMR and TDEE in kcal/day (Mifflin-St Jeor times an activity factor); `calculate_macro_targets(weight_kg, goal, tdee_kcal)` returns the kcal target, the kcal range the sources give for the goal, protein, carbohydrate and fat (g, % of kcal, g/kg), the protein range, whether the floor was applied, notes and the sources. Arguments are checked by `TdeeArgs` / `MacroArgs` (Pydantic, strict numbers, the profile ranges); bad input gives a result with status "invalid" and a fixed message naming the fields. `FORMULA_SOURCES` holds the credit for #22 and #23.

- **Why:** the owner wanted every number sourced. None of the 21 approved articles gives the Mifflin-St Jeor equation or activity factors, so two CC BY articles that state them in their methods were found through the Europe PMC REST API and approved (copyright rule 4). The point targets sit at the cautious end of the source ranges (slower loss, smaller surplus) and the tool also returns the ranges, so the model can explain the choice. The tools are pure functions with no Streamlit import, like the food lookup, so T4.4 only wraps them.
- **Decisions & gotchas:**
  - Owner's choices (2026-10-19): sources #22 and #23; floor 1200 kcal/day for both sexes (the top of the low-energy diet range, 800-1200 kcal/day, in #1 and #9: the sources define the range, using its top as a floor is the project's choice); lose fat 0.5%/week; build muscle +10%; protein 2.2 g/kg for fat loss, 1.9 otherwise; fat 25% of kcal; when protein and fat do not fit, lower protein with a note.
  - Rounding: BMR and TDEE are rounded once, at the end (the TDEE multiplies the exact BMR). Carbohydrate grams (and protein when lowered) round up, so the macro kcal add up to the target or up to 3 kcal more, never less: the floor holds for the grams too. Percentages are of the kcal target, so they may not add up to exactly 100.
  - The floor also lifts targets above the estimated needs for small, older, sedentary profiles (lowest TDEE in range: 467 kcal/day; e.g. 45 kg, 150 cm, 70 years, sedentary: 1052). Then the message says the target was raised to the floor instead of naming the goal's method, and a note says the target is at or above the estimated needs (for "lose fat": so it will not lead to fat loss) and points to a registered dietitian. When the floor only shrinks a deficit, the note says so. `floor_applied` is True only when the rounded estimate was below the floor.
  - `MACRO_TDEE_KCAL_RANGE` (400-9000) covers every TDEE the profile ranges allow (467-8270, tested), so a TDEE from `calculate_tdee` is always accepted.
  - The lowered-protein case only happens for very heavy bodies with a low target (e.g. 300 kg losing fat on a TDEE of 3000); ordinary profiles never reach it.
  - Not used on purpose: #11's ~45 kcal/kg (an average reported intake of off-season bodybuilders, not a recommendation; copyright rule 7).
