## 2026-10-19 · T4.3 USDA food table and lookup · #28 (closes #13)
- **Brief:** core requirement 2 (the third tool).
- **What:**
  - `scripts/prepare_usda.py` (new): reads `food.csv` and `food_nutrient.csv` straight from the Foundation Foods CSV zip (no unpacking, no network) and writes `data/usda_foods.csv`: FDC ID, description, and per-100 g kcal, protein, carbohydrate, fat and fiber, with USDA's suggested credit, the CC0 notice, "no endorsement" and the changes made as `#` lines on top.
  - `data/usda_foods.csv` (new, committed, CC0): 328 foods from release 2026-04-30.
  - `src/tools/food.py` (new): `load_food_table()` (validated, cached), `FoodLookupArgs` (Pydantic, strict: a name of 2-100 characters with at least one word of 2 or more letters or digits once punctuation is dropped, checked by a field validator; a finite number of grams from 0.1 to 2000), `lookup_food_nutrition(food, grams)` returning a `FoodLookupResult` with `status` found / not_found / invalid, the scaled nutrients, the matched USDA name and FDC ID, up to 3 alternatives or suggestions, a message and the source.
  - `src/config.py`: `PROJECT_ROOT` (the same line as T2.1's), USDA paths, the zip member size cap, the USDA credit text (`USDA_CITATION`, `USDA_LICENSE`, `USDA_CHANGES`, shared by the table and `ATTRIBUTION.md`), the lookup limits and match thresholds.
  - `ATTRIBUTION.md`: a "Food data" section credits USDA FoodData Central (citation, release, CC0 link, changes, no endorsement). `render_attribution()` takes the USDA release, which `scripts/generate_attribution.py` reads from the table's credit line (`read_usda_release()` in `src/tools/food.py`); the existing test that the committed file equals the generated one now covers it.
  - Tests: `tests/test_prepare_usda.py` (made-up fixture CSVs in `tests/fixtures/usda/`, zipped at test time), `tests/test_food_lookup.py`. 776 tests pass (with `main` merged in: T2.1, T1.3, T2.2 and T4.1).
  - Docs: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 6 (nutrient IDs, cleaning, matching), `docs/SOURCES.md` #21 (release in use, CC0 read on the home page), `docs/SECURITY.md` (tool arguments, the USDA zip), `docs/TESTING.md` (the real run and how 40 food names matched), `CLAUDE.md` (command), plan (T4.3 in review).
- **Why:** the table is small and CC0, so it is committed (D7) and the app and tests never need the network. Matching was tuned on the real table: `WRatio` alone or `token_set_ratio` alone each gave wrong foods ("tuna" -> butter, "banana" -> banana peppers), so the score is their mean, and ties are broken by USDA's "Head, modifier" naming and by preferring the plain food over a processed form. Results are data for the model, so invalid input gets a fixed message that does not echo what was sent.
- **Decisions & gotchas:**
  - Owner's choices (2026-10-19): Foundation Foods only (SR Legacy not added); energy from Atwater specific factors first; Claude downloaded the zip once (into gitignored `data/usda/`); this branch starts from `main` in its own worktree because T2.1 and T4.1 were in progress in the main checkout.
  - Energy is nutrient 2048 (Atwater specific factors) when present, then 2047 (general factors: 4, 9, 4 kcal per g), then 1008 (312 foods have both 2047 and 2048, 35 only 2047, 31 only 1008, which mostly matches 2048). Owner's choice after the code review (2026-10-19): specific factors are more exact per food (raw almonds 584 kcal instead of 626, creamy peanut butter 589 instead of 632) and closer to 1008; fiber is 1079, then 2033. Foods without energy or a macronutrient are left out (92 in this release, including olive, canola and the other cooking oils except coconut, butter and dry pasta); a missing fiber value is empty ("not reported"), never 0; negative carbohydrate-by-difference values become 0; a name repeated across releases keeps its latest publication, which can be one without fiber (e.g. strawberries).
  - Known weak matches on the real table (recorded in `docs/TESTING.md`): "egg" returns a raw yolk first (the whole egg is an alternative), "olive oil" and "butter" match foods that contain them, a bare "chicken" returns a wing with skin and "rice" black rice (chosen by name, not nutrition; the tool description asks the model for a specific cut or form), "pasta" matches pasta sauce and "oatmeal" oatmeal cookies (dry pasta and plain oatmeal are not in the table), typos below the threshold are "not found" with the right suggestion. The model sees the matched name and the alternatives and can look up a better one.
