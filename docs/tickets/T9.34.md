### T9.34 Foods the coach suggests to a client, logged by the client in one click (M, extra) · status: in progress · issue NMH1988/fitcoach-ai-assistant#117
**Serves:** Sprint 3 (`docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md`): evaluation: problem definition, in part (how the app addresses the coach's problem: the coach guides what the client eats, not only the targets); a reviewer's comment of 2026-11-12 that a gymer choosing all their food alone is weak and the coach should recommend foods (to be recorded in `docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md` in the owner's words); Sprint 2 (`docs/BRIEF.md`): none; beyond the brief (owner's request): the feature itself, which neither brief asks for.
**Depends on:** T9.13, T9.15, T9.17
**Acceptance criteria** (proposed and approved by the owner on 2026-11-12)
- [x] **The store keeps a coach's list of suggested foods for a client.** A list is a set of items, each a meal slot (`breakfast`, `lunch`, `dinner`, `snack`, as `meal_items`), a food from the USDA table (`fdc_id` and its name) and grams within the food log's range (`config.FOOD_MIN_GRAMS` to `config.FOOD_MAX_GRAMS`), at most `config.MAX_SUGGESTED_FOODS` (20) items, a food at most once per meal slot; an empty list clears the suggestions. Each list names the coach who wrote it. A coach changes the list by saving a new one (records are never updated in place); the client's newest list is the active one. The tables come with migration 6 (`schema._V6`; `_V1` to `_V5` are never edited), `STRICT`, foreign keys on and never cascading - `tests/test_store_db.py` (an upgrade from each of versions 1 to 5 keeps every row), store unit tests (a value out of range raises `StoreValueError` naming the field, never the value). Done: `schema._V6`, `src/store/suggestions.py`; `tests/test_store_db.py` (upgrades from 1 to 5, a failed migration 6 rolls back), `tests/test_store_suggestions.py`.
- [x] **Only the client's coach writes it, and only while the client shares nutrition.** One new store function (e.g. `suggestions.add_client_suggestions(conn, coach_id, client_id, items)`) runs `access.authorise_read(conn, coach_id, client_id, "nutrition")` inside its write transaction, with the insert and an audit row with no values (the new action `suggestions_set`, added to `access.Action` and to the `audit_log.action` CHECK by rebuilding `audit_log` in migration 6, as `schema._V5` did); otherwise it raises `NotFound` and writes no row and no audit row. It joins the group of a coach's writes (`COACH_WRITES`) in `tests/test_store_access.py`, run through every refused pair (`REFUSED`), the coach's own ID, and a client who shares body and training but not nutrition - store unit tests; `tests/test_store_db.py` (`access.Action` equals the CHECK; migration 6 keeps the indexes and the trigger of `audit_log`). Done: `suggestions.add_client_suggestions()`; `COACH_WRITES` in `tests/test_store_access.py`.
- [x] **Who reads it.** The gymer reads their own active list: the newest list written by the coach they are connected with now, so a former coach's list is hidden once the relationship ends, though kept in the store and the export (owner's choice, 2026-11-12); the coach reads the newest list they wrote for a client only through `access.authorised_read()` (an audited `read`) while the relationship is active and nutrition is shared, in the refused-cases matrix; anyone else gets the same `NotFound`. The gymer has no path that writes, changes or deletes the list; only `my_data.delete_user_data()` removes it, for either side (owner's choice: the list names its coach) - store unit tests. Done: `suggestions.current_suggestions()` in `READS`; the former and new coach tests in `tests/test_store_suggestions.py`.
- [x] **The gymer sees the suggestions on Today and logs one in a click.** A section "Suggested by your coach" lists the active list by meal slot, with grams, energy and macros from the USDA table, marked as the coach's suggestions and read-only; each item has a control that adds it to the picked day's food log with the suggested meal slot and grams; the gymer may change the grams before adding, and the meal slot afterwards with Today's edit (owner's choice, 2026-11-12, so a list of 20 foods does not carry 40 inputs). The gymer still logs any other food as before; with no active list, the section does not show. English and German - AppTest. Done: `views/suggestions.py`; `tests/test_app_suggestions.py` (English and German), checked by hand in the browser.
- [x] **A logged item remembers that it came from a suggestion.** `meal_items` gains a flag `from_suggestion` (0 or 1, default 0; migration 6, `ALTER TABLE ... ADD COLUMN`), set only by the one-click path, which checks that the item is on the gymer's active list; an item edited to another food no longer counts as from a suggestion, one edited with the same food keeps it - store unit tests. A flag, not the suggestion item's ID (owner's choice, 2026-11-12): a list goes when its coach deletes their data, which a reference would block or leave pointing at nothing. Done: `records.add_meal_item(suggestion_item_id=)`, `replace_meal_item()`; `tests/test_store_suggestions.py`.
- [x] **The coach builds the list on Client week.** Beside the target form (T9.17), only while the client shares nutrition: rows of meal slot, food (from the USDA table) and grams, added and removed before saving; saving writes a new list; the form shows the list's total energy and macros next to the client's active target, as information. Client week also shows, for the week shown, how much of what the client logged came from the suggestions (items and share of energy), worked out in `src/metrics.py` - metrics unit tests, AppTest (English and German). Done: `views/suggestions.py`, `metrics.suggested_share()` (from the relationship's start, compliance run 1); `tests/test_app_suggestions.py`, `tests/test_suggested_foods.py`.
- [x] **A list of foods to choose from, not a meal plan.** The calorie floor is not applied to the list's total, as the list is not a day's intake; both pages say so in one sentence, and say that the client may eat other foods (the PRD's guardrails apply to what the gymer logs and to the targets, as today) - AppTest on the texts. Done: the texts on both pages, `tests/test_app_suggestions.py`.
- [x] **Nothing new reaches a model.** In this ticket the suggestions and the share logged from them are not added to `get_client_week`, the weekly review's metrics or any prompt - a test that a coach's chat turn and a review run on a client with a canary suggestion carry it in no message (`tests/test_agent_clients.py`, `tests/test_weekly_review.py`). Done: a whole coach turn (`tests/test_agent_clients.py`) and a whole review run (`tests/test_agent_review.py`) with a canary list.
- [x] **Logs, export and delete.** No log line holds a food, a gram value or a list (`tests/test_observability.py`, canary values through the new store functions); the client's export holds the lists about them and their items, a hidden former coach's included, and the `suggestions_set` and `read` audit rows are in both sides' exports (`my_data._AUDIT`); the coach's export does not hold the lists (the client's data); "Delete everything" by either side removes the lists between them and their items, naming every new table in `my_data.delete_user_data()`; the client's food log keeps its flags - `tests/test_store_my_data.py`. Done: `tests/test_observability.py`, `tests/test_store_my_data.py`.
- [x] **The gymer is told.** A new notification kind (T9.29's list), "your coach updated your suggested foods", for each list saved about the gymer: its time and the coach's display name only, never a food or a gram value - `tests/test_store_notifications.py`, AppTest of the text (English and German) (owner's choice, 2026-11-12). Done: `notifications.py` (`suggestions_updated`); `tests/test_store_notifications.py`, `tests/test_views_tables.py`, the Notifications AppTest.
- [x] **Docs match the code.** `docs/PRD.md`: feature 29 and AC-44, the gymer's role ("reads what the coach suggests"), the privacy section (the second write of a coach into a client's records), and "Moved to the Capstone" (meal plans: a coach-built plan whose foods the client may swap, built on this list; T9.26's gymer-drafted plan recorded as replaced or kept, by the owner's choice); `.claude/rules/security-privacy.md` ("The store": the new function, its access-test group and `suggestions_set` among the audited actions), `docs/SECURITY.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (section 16), the README's use cases, and the reviewer's comment in `docs/BRIEF_SPRINT3.md` - review; the `compliance-reviewer` on the PR (a coach writing into a client's records, `.claude/rules/workflow.md`). Done: the PRD, rules, SECURITY, ARCHITECTURE, README, BRIEF_SPRINT3 and CLAUDE.md; compliance run 1 recorded in the PROGRESS entry.
