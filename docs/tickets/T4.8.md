**Acceptance criteria** (approved by the owner, 2026-10-22; choice: only foods in the list, no free typing)
- [x] The Food lookup tab has a searchable dropdown (`st.selectbox`, typing filters the list) with every food in the USDA table (sorted, one entry per food), with no food selected at the start - AppTest (`tests/test_app_targets.py`: `test_the_food_dropdown_lists_every_food_sorted_with_none_chosen`, `test_every_food_of_the_real_table_can_be_chosen_and_looked_up`); unit test on `food_names` (`tests/test_food_lookup.py`); checked in the browser (2026-10-22: typing "egg" lists the egg foods).

- [x] Without a food chosen, Look up is disabled and nothing is looked up - AppTest (`test_look_up_is_disabled_until_a_food_is_chosen`).
- [x] A food whose name is longer than `FOOD_MAX_NAME_CHARS` (2 of the 328 foods) can be chosen and looked up - unit tests (`test_a_name_longer_than_the_free_text_limit_can_be_chosen`, `test_every_food_of_the_committed_table_is_found_by_its_own_name`) and the AppTest on the real table above.
- [x] The tab's placeholder and caption say to pick a food from the list, in English and German; food names stay English - AppTest (`tests/test_app_targets.py`: `test_the_food_dropdown_lists_every_food_sorted_with_none_chosen` for English; `tests/test_app_language.py`: `test_the_food_lookup_is_german_with_english_food_names`, `test_an_invalid_lookup_shows_the_german_message`).
- [x] The chat tool `lookup_food_nutrition` and the meal plan check keep their free-text behaviour - their tests are unchanged (`tests/test_food_lookup.py`, `tests/test_meal_plan.py`, `tests/test_tools_registry.py`).
- [x] `docs/UI_DESIGN.md` updated; ruff and the whole test suite pass (full suite run, then the affected files again after the last fix).
