- [x] The notifications are worked out from rows the store already holds, with no copy of any value: a coach sees "<client> connected" (an invitation accepted, with the scopes shared at the start), "<client> started / stopped sharing <scope>" for each change after the start, and "Your relationship with <client> ended"; a gymer sees "You are connected with <coach>", "<coach> approved your weekly review for the week of <Monday>" (only an approved review about them, never a pending or rejected one) and "Your relationship with <coach> ended".
